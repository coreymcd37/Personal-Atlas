# How we treat each other

This is my project, and I want strangers to be able to work on it without getting talked down to.

If you are here, you are welcome. That includes people who are new to code, people who are changing the globe for their own family, and people who are fixing a bug I left in.

## I ask for this

- Talk about the work, not the person.
- Assume the other person is trying to help.
- Say what is wrong and what you would like instead.
- No harassment, no slurs, no sexual comments, no piling on.
- Do not post someone else's private life, address, or travel in a way they did not agree to. My places are mine to share. Yours are yours.

## If something goes wrong

Write me on a GitHub issue, or use the private contact on my GitHub profile if you do not want it public. Tell me what happened and where. I will answer, and I will remove comments or contributions that break this.

This applies to issues, pull requests, and any other space I set up for this project.
